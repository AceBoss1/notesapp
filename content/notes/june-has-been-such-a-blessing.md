My audience consisted of Doctors, Nurses, Biomedical Scientists, Bankers, Lawyers, Students, Engineers, Business owners, Managers etc. I ensured to speak to everyone  in a language that made sense to where they were at the time.
I absolutely love doing data - all of the cleaning, sorting and filtering, wrangling and formating, modelling and relationships, Power pivots and queries, V and X lookups, Visualising with Power-BI, Lux, Matplotlib and Seaborn or keeping it simple with Excel dashboards, employing built in AIs where need be and creating visuals that  tell meaningful stories while providing insights. I love the thrill that comes with having to reject or failing to uphold a hypothesis based on statistical revelations. 
I love all of that.

Having spent the last couple of weekends talking to hundreads of people about data, I have come to the conclusion that talking about data is just as exciting as doing data.  Sharing my knowledge and insights with colleagues, friends, acquaintances and even total strangers has been such a blessing.
Being part of the success story of many who I may never meet in person has been an honor. 
Talking, sharing, connecting. 
This month has shown me that the little you know might be a big deal to someone else and the more you share knowledge, you leave an indelible print of you in the hearts of people and collectively we make the world a better place.
My verbal and non verbal communication skills, presentation and delivery as well as audience capture capacity has grown and I am thrilled!!!
June has been such a blessing!
I am a self-motivated Data Analyst and Communicator. I am evolving and I am so loving me.
Wishing you a fantastic week filled with blessings and so much love 

❤️ 😍
