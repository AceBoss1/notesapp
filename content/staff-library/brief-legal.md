# Legal and compliance brief

**Legal & compliance keeps NotesApp trustworthy: members' data safe, young members protected, money handled lawfully, and every promise we make in writing kept.** Trust is our product as much as journals or payouts are.

## The rules that shape us

- **Data:** the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: a lawful basis for everything we hold, only what we need, members' rights to see, correct, export and delete, and 72 hours to tell the regulator about a serious breach.
- **Young members:** teens and children get protections by design; a child's data needs a parent's consent; nothing collected while young is reused for adult marketing without fresh consent.
- **Money:** payments, payouts, giving and tickets run through licensed partners (Paystack, Paylony); bonus credits and game money (N$) are never cashed out.
- **Content:** takedowns for copyright, defamation and impersonation; our terms say what's allowed and how to appeal.
- **Employment:** Nigerian labour law for staff; options only for contractors who are not employees; Nigerian counsel for the stock options sub-plan.

## Principles

- **Written, templated, reviewed:** every contract starts from a library template, and only the CEO or CFO signs.
- **No informal answers** to the police, courts, lawyers or regulators: Legal answers, in writing, sharing only what the law requires.
- **Say what's true in public:** our terms, privacy policy, prices and commissions match the product exactly.
