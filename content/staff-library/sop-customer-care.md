# SOP: Customer care, trust & safety

**Purpose:** help every member quickly and kindly, and keep NotesApp safe, above all for young members. **Owners:** the Customer care team lead and the Trust & safety team lead; the Community Strategist owns communities.

## Routines

- **Daily:** contact messages, leads and feedback in the team hub; the reports queue; anything Nana's help centre couldn't answer.
- **Cover hours:** the hours this department must cover are set by the team lead on the rota in the team hub; first replies within one working day, urgent safety reports within the hour.
- **Weekly:** the top five reasons members wrote in, sent to Product with the members' own words.

## Procedures

### Answering a member

1. Read the help article for their question first; link it in your answer.
2. Look the member up only if you must, through the team hub; showing a masked email or phone needs a reason, and every view is logged.
3. Answer in plain words, in naira, with what happens next and when. Never promise a refund or a change you can't make yourself.
4. If it needs another team, assign a task in the team hub and tell the member when they'll hear back.

### Refunds and money problems

| Situation | The rule members see |
| --- | --- |
| A booked session that didn't happen | The member reports a problem; money is held until it's settled |
| A course not started | Refundable within 7 days of buying |
| A Naija Exam Prep pack | The 7-day refund rule |
| A boost cancelled | Half of what's left is refunded |
| An order not received | Held until delivery; raise it with the seller, then Finance |

Pass every refund to Finance as a team hub task; Finance confirms before you tell the member it's done.

### A report (content, account, scam)

1. Trust & safety reviews it against the terms: remove, restrict, warn, suspend or keep.
2. Tell the reporter it was handled and the member what was done and how to appeal, without naming the reporter.
3. An appeal is reviewed by someone other than the person who made the first decision.
4. Copyright, defamation and impersonation complaints go to Legal & compliance with the report.

### Young members

1. Any sign a child or teen may be at risk (grooming, self-harm, abuse, an adult asking to meet): act within the hour. Restrict the account or content, keep the evidence, and tell the founder and Legal.
2. Never contact the young member outside the product, and never share what you saw in team chats beyond the people handling it.
3. Teen and kids protections are never switched off for anyone, for any reason.

### Data requests

A member asking to see, correct, export or delete their data: confirm the account, then pass it to Legal & compliance as a task the same day.

### Communities (the Community Strategist)

The full procedures are in the SOP: Community Strategist. In short:

The Community Strategist works in Customer care and helps churches, mosques, NGOs, schools, creators and businesses run healthy communities on NotesApp (/c/name).

1. **A new community:** within 2 working days, welcome its admins, share the onboarding checklist (rules, roles, moderators, a first event), and add it to the team hub work board.
2. **Giving and tithes:** before a community's first giving appeal is promoted, check its organisation verification with Trust & safety. Scams and look-alike appeals go straight to Trust & safety.
3. **Weekly:** review the most active and the quiet communities; offer help to the quiet ones, and plan lives, audio rooms and series with active hosts.
4. **Requests and problems:** what admins ask for goes to Product as a team hub task in their own words. A conflict between members goes to Trust & safety; the Strategist never settles it alone.
5. **Monthly:** a short report to the Customer care team lead: active communities, first events held, and the top requests.

Measures: communities active each week (a post, a live or a room); new communities holding a first event within 30 days; admins' satisfaction, each quarter.

## Measures

- First reply within one working day (target: 9 in 10).
- Safety reports involving young members acted on within the hour (target: all).
- Appeals decided within 5 working days.

## When to escalate

- A risk to a child: the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder and Legal, the same hour.
- Fraud or a pattern of scams: Finance and Engineering, the same day.
- A complaint that may go to the press or a regulator: the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO and Legal.
