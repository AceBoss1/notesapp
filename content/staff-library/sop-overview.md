# #NotesApp SOP Manuals

Nov 3, 2026 · Emmanuel Adams, Founder, CEO & CTO #NotesApp Inc.

## How to use these manuals

Each department has one SOP manual, in its own tab here and, once approved, in the team hub library at /admin, where everyone in the department ticks **I have read this**. The Employee Handbook says how we work as a company; your SOP says how your department does its job, step by step.

**Every SOP follows the same shape:**

1. **Purpose and owner:** what the department is for and which team lead owns the manual.
2. **Routines:** what happens daily, weekly and monthly.
3. **Procedures:** numbered steps for each recurring job, with who does it and where in the team hub.
4. **Approvals:** who signs off what. Nobody approves their own work, time, leave or spending.
5. **Measures:** the few numbers that show the department is doing its job.
6. **When to escalate:** what goes to the team lead, the founder or Legal, and how fast.

**Keeping them right:** the team lead reviews their SOP every quarter, and whenever a release changes how the department works. Changes are announced in the team hub and re-ticked. If an SOP and reality differ, follow the safer of the two and tell your team lead the same day.

**Nana-HR** answers staff questions from these manuals and the handbook, and always names the SOP and step she used.

## Shared procedures every department follows

### Work and approvals

1. Every piece of work is a task on the team hub work board, with an owner, a department and a due date. Work that isn't on the board isn't assigned.
2. Requests for a leadership decision or meeting go to the Chief of Staff in the team hub, with a due date.
3. Spending and contracts are approved only by the CEO or the CFO, in the team hub, before any commitment. Nobody approves their own spending, time or leave.
4. Announcements to the whole team go out from the team hub, never from a personal channel.

### When something goes wrong

| What happened | Do this at once | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member data seen, sent or lost where it shouldn't be | Tell your team lead and Legal & compliance in the team hub | Legal decides within 24 hours whether the regulator (72 hours) and members must be told |
| A risk to a child or young member | Tell Trust & safety and the founder the same hour | Trust & safety follows its SOP; never contact the young person outside the product |
| Money paid wrongly or missing | Tell Finance in the team hub | Finance follows its SOP; never promise a member a refund before Finance confirms |
| The site or a feature is down | Check the public status page, then tell Engineering in the team hub | Customer care answers members with the agreed wording |
| A security alarm (a strange login, a phishing message) | Don't click; tell Engineering and your team lead | Engineering checks and tells you what to do |

### Data and legal requests

Any request from a member, the police, a court, a lawyer or a regulator about member data goes to Legal & compliance the same day, unanswered. Nobody else replies to it.

### Releases

When a release changes how a department works, its team lead updates their SOP within a week, and the change is announced in the team hub for everyone to re-tick.

### Handovers

Before leave or leaving, hand each open task to a named colleague on the work board, with a short note: where it stands, what's next, who's waiting.
