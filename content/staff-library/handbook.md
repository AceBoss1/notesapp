# #NotesApp Employee Handbook

Nov 3, 2026 · Emmanuel Adams, Founder, CEO & CTO #NotesApp Inc.

## Welcome

Welcome to #NotesApp. This handbook is how we work: what we expect of you, what you can expect of us, and where to go when you need something. It applies to everyone who works for NOTESAPP TECHNOLOGIES LTD (RC 9825642), the operating company of the NotesApp group, whatever their role or department. Contract staff follow the parts on conduct, data, security and confidentiality; the parts on employment (hours, pay, leave, notice) apply to employees. Your written contract or offer letter sits beside this handbook; where the two differ, your contract wins.

- **Where it lives:** in the team hub library at /admin (Staff login in the site footer, or the NotesApp Teamhub app installed from your browser). Read it in your first week and tick **I have read this**.
- **The SOP manual for your department** sits in the same library. The handbook says how we work as a company; your SOP says how your department does its job, step by step.
- **How it changes:** only the founder or someone the founder names changes this handbook. Every change is announced in the team hub, and you are asked to read and tick it again.
- **Questions:** ask Nana-HR in the team hub first; she answers from this handbook and the SOPs and says which section she used. Anything she can't answer goes to your team lead, then to the C-suite, then to the founder.

## Joining us

### How we hire

1. **The role is approved** by the founder, with its department, team lead, hourly rate range and weekly hours.
2. **We post it** on our careers page; applications come into the team hub.
3. **Nana-HR summarises** each application against the role's requirements. She never rejects or ranks anyone on her own: a person reads every application that meets the stated requirements.
4. **Interviews** are held on NotesApp calls: a first conversation with the team lead, a short paid or practical task where the role needs one, and a final conversation with the founder or someone the founder names.
5. **References and checks** are taken only with the candidate's consent.
6. **The offer** is drafted from our template, checked by Legal & compliance and signed before the first day.

### Who can work with us

- You are 18 or over and legally able to work in Nigeria.
- You have a NIN and a Nigerian bank account in your own name, for pay.
- You have a phone or computer and an internet connection good enough for your role (see Working remotely).

### Your contract and first months

- **A written offer letter or contract before your first day,** stating your role, who you report to, your hours, your pay or fees, your pay day, your notice period, and the confidentiality and intellectual property terms. Your employer is NOTESAPP TECHNOLOGIES LTD; if you also receive stock options, they come separately from our parent company, NotesApp Inc. (see Pay).
- **How you're engaged** is one of three ways, written in your letter: an employee on salary; an employee on salary with stock options; or a contractor whose reward is stock options only. Contractors are not employees: their consulting agreement sets their work and terms.
- **Probation (employees):** your first 3 months. Your team lead checks in with you every month, and at the end you are confirmed, given up to 3 more months with clear goals, or let go with the notice in your contract.
- **Your first week:** your staff login and departments, this handbook and your SOP read and ticked, your first tasks on the work board, and a call with your team lead.

### Equal opportunity

We hire, pay and promote on the work alone. Nobody is treated less fairly because of their ethnic group, state of origin, religion, sex, disability, marital status or age. Tell us before an interview or at any time if you need an adjustment to do your best work, and we'll make it where we reasonably can.

## Working hours and Time Track

**Your weekly hours are in your contract. The standard week is 20 hours: 4 a day, Monday to Friday; some roles are fewer (10 hours a week, for example).** Everyone logs their hours in NotesApp Time Track, in the team hub. For staff paid by the hour, approved hours are paid at your hourly rate; for salaried staff, they show you worked your contracted week. We track hours only: no screenshots, no keystrokes, nothing but the hours you log.

### Logging your time

- Start the timer on what you're working on: a work item, a project, a meeting or other work. Add time by hand if you forgot, with a short note.
- A running timer asks **"still working?"** every 30 minutes. If you don't answer within 10 minutes, it stops at your last answer, so the last 30 minutes don't count. A meeting timer waits for the meeting's planned length before it first asks.
- A timer still running at midnight (Lagos time) stops there and asks you to check it.
- Your first time in after 9:00 am Lagos time is marked late. Lateness is noted, but your hours are what you're paid for.

### Your weekly timesheet

1. Check your week before Friday afternoon; a reminder comes at 4:00 pm on Friday if it isn't submitted.
2. Submit it. Once submitted, it's locked; ask your team lead to return it if something needs fixing.
3. Your team lead (or a super admin) approves it. Nobody approves their own timesheet.
4. Approved hours go into the month's payroll.

### Availability

We work remotely across Nigeria, so your 4 hours a day are yours to place, with three exceptions: meetings you've accepted, the hours your SOP says your role must cover (for example, customer care's response hours), and urgent issues your team lead asks you to join. Tell your team lead in the team hub if you can't work a day you're due.

**Extra hours** are worked only when your team lead has asked for them on the work board first. Hours you log beyond your contracted week without that approval may not be approved.

## Pay

**How you're paid depends on how you're engaged, and your offer letter says which.** Pay, equity and bonuses are confidential to the person involved: never share or ask about someone else's, and they never appear in staff-wide messages.

| Arrangement | Cash | Stock options | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Salary | A monthly salary, paid in arrears; or approved hours × an hourly rate | None | Most employees |
| Salary and options | As above | An option grant from NotesApp Inc. under its 2026 Equity Incentive Plan | Leaders and key hires |
| Options only | None from us | An option grant from NotesApp Inc. | Mostly contract staff who are not employees |

- **Salary or rate:** in your offer letter. An employee's pay is never below the national minimum wage for the hours worked. Some offers include a deferred bonus or a pay rise tied to a company milestone; your letter states exactly when it is paid and when it lapses.
- **Pay day** is the day in your letter (the 28th unless it says otherwise). If it falls on a weekend or public holiday, you're paid on the working day before.
- **How you're paid:** by bank transfer to the Nigerian account in your own name. Your full account number is stored encrypted; staff only ever see its last four digits.
- **What you can see:** your hours in Time, your monthly pay statement from Finance, and Nana-HR's answers about your own pay and options, never anyone else's.
- **Deductions:** only those the law requires (income tax under PAYE, pension and other statutory contributions that apply to you) or that you agree to in writing. Every deduction is on your statement.
- **A mistake in your pay:** tell Finance in the team hub within 30 days. An underpayment is corrected in the next run at the latest; an overpayment is agreed with you before anything is taken back.
- **Leaving part way through a month:** you're paid for the part you worked.

### Stock options

Stock options are a benefit from our parent company, NotesApp Inc. (a Delaware corporation), under its 2026 Equity Incentive Plan. They are not part of your salary and not a term of your employment contract; your own Option Grant Agreement and the Plan govern them.

- **What an option is:** the right to buy a set number of NotesApp Inc. shares at a fixed exercise price, set at no less than fair market value on the day the Board grants it.
- **How it vests:** as your grant says, usually partly over time (monthly, after a cliff) and partly as the company reaches milestones such as 1, 10, 50 and 200 million verified registered users and a revenue milestone. Milestones are verified independently of you, and any not reached within five years of the grant lapse.
- **When your service ends, for any reason:** unvested options end at once. You keep only what has vested, and you have 14 days to exercise it, or it ends.
- **Before a Series A:** you get at least 14 days' notice by email and a team hub bell, and may exercise vested options in that window.
- **The rules:** options can't be sold, transferred or pledged; shares you buy carry the company's bylaws, a right of first refusal and the Voting Agreement; you pay the exercise price (in US dollars or another way the Board allows) and your own taxes, and we recommend independent tax advice.
- **Questions:** Nana-HR answers from your own grant and the Plan; the Board Secretary handles exercise notices.

### Expenses

Spend company money only when your team lead has approved it in the team hub first. Keep the receipt, upload it with your claim, and Finance repays approved claims with the next pay run. We don't repay personal phone or data costs unless your contract includes an allowance.

## Leave and time off

Employees get leave as the law provides, pro rata to their contracted hours, and more where their contract says so. Request every kind of leave in the team hub (Time → Leave), where your team lead or a super admin approves it; nobody approves their own. Contractors agree time away with their team lead.

| Kind | How much | How to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Annual leave | At least the legal minimum (6 working days a year after 12 months' continuous service), or what your contract gives, pro rata to your hours | At least 2 weeks ahead for 3 or more days; 2 working days ahead for less |
| Sick leave | Up to 12 working days a year on full pay, with a medical certificate for 2 or more days in a row | Tell your team lead as early as you can that day, then log it in the team hub |
| Public holidays | The public holidays the Federal Government declares | Set for the whole team in the team hub; nothing to request |
| Maternity leave | At least 12 weeks, as the Labour Act provides, with your job kept for you | Tell your team lead and the founder as early as you're comfortable, ideally 8 weeks ahead |
| Paternity, compassionate and other leave | As agreed with your team lead and the founder | Request it as Other leave with a short note |

**On leave you are off.** You don't log hours, answer messages or join meetings; set your team hub status and hand your open tasks to a named colleague on the work board before you go. Approved leave counts towards your attendance.

**If you're unwell while working remotely,** stop the timer and rest. Hours you didn't work are not logged, and sick leave covers paid days as above.

## Working remotely across Nigeria

We are a remote team. You can work from anywhere in Nigeria, and from time to time we may ask you to meet at a place the company names, with travel agreed and paid in advance.

- **Your setup:** a phone or computer you can keep secure, and an internet connection good enough for calls. Plan around power cuts and data: a power bank or backup, and a second network if your area is unreliable.
- **Test like a member:** check your work on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data, because that's how most members use NotesApp.
- **Be reachable in your hours:** keep the team hub open (or the NotesApp Teamhub app installed) while your timer is running, and answer messages from your team within your working day.
- **Meetings:** on NotesApp calls or audio rooms, with your camera on when you can. Join on time; if you can't make it, say so before it starts.
- **Where you work:** a place where nobody can see or hear member data or confidential calls. Never work on member data in a public place or on a shared screen.
- **Moving or travelling:** tell your team lead if you'll work from a different state for more than a week. Working from outside Nigeria needs the founder's written approval first, because of data and tax rules.
- **Company equipment,** if we give you any, stays ours: look after it, don't lend it, and return it when you leave.

## Conduct

We treat each other, our members and our partners the way we want to be treated: with respect, honesty and patience.

### How we work together

- **Write it down in the team hub:** decisions, requests and handovers go on the work board or in the message hub, not in private chats, so anyone can pick them up.
- **Be clear and kind:** short, plain messages; say what you need and by when. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- **No harassment, bullying or discrimination,** online or offline, including jokes about someone's ethnic group, religion, sex, disability or background. This applies in team chats, calls and NotesApp itself.
- **Own your mistakes:** say what went wrong, what you've done about it, and what will stop it happening again. We fix problems; we don't hunt for someone to blame.

### Conflicts of interest and gifts

- Tell your team lead in writing if you, your family or a close friend has an interest in a partner, supplier, advertiser or member account you deal with, and step back from decisions about it.
- Don't accept gifts, money or favours from partners, suppliers or members beyond small courtesies (a branded item, a meal at a meeting). Anything worth more than ₦10,000 is declared in the team hub.
- Side work is fine if it doesn't compete with NotesApp, use our time or information, or affect your hours. Tell your team lead before you start.

### Social media and speaking for NotesApp

- Only the founder and the people they name speak for NotesApp to the press, investors or partners.
- You're welcome to say you work at NotesApp and to share our public posts. Don't share anything that isn't public: plans, numbers, member stories, screenshots of the team hub or member data.
- Follow the brand guide at /brand whenever you use our name or marks.

### Confidentiality and intellectual property

- Company information stays inside the team hub and our approved tools, during your work and after you leave.
- What you create in your work (writing, designs, code, documents and know-how) belongs to the company, which may pass it to NotesApp Inc., the group's intellectual property holder.
- Pay, equity, funding and financial figures are confidential to the people involved and never go in staff-wide messages.

## Members' data, security and young members

Members trust us with their money, their words and their children. Breaking that trust is serious misconduct.

### Members' data

- We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Look at a member's data only when your work needs it, and only through the team hub.
- Members' emails and phone numbers are masked; showing one needs a reason, and every view is logged. Never copy member data out of the team hub: no spreadsheets, screenshots or personal notes.
- Never share member data with anyone outside the company, including the police or a lawyer, without Legal & compliance. Legal answers every data or legal request.
- If a member asks to see, correct or delete their data, pass it to Customer care, who follow the SOP.

### Security

- Protect your staff login with a passkey or security key where the team hub offers it, and never share your login. Each person has their own.
- Lock your device when you step away, keep it updated, and don't install software you don't trust on the device you work on.
- Be suspicious of any message asking for a access to your device, code, a password, a payment or a change of bank details, even one that seems to come from the founder. Check in the team hub first.
- **Report anything wrong at once,** in the team hub to your team lead and Legal & compliance: a lost device, a message sent to the wrong person, data seen by someone who shouldn't have seen it. We have 72 hours to tell the data regulator about a serious breach, so minutes matter. **Reporting quickly is never punished.**

### Young members

- Teens (13 to 17) and children (under 13, on a parent's account) use NotesApp with protections built in. Never weaken them, never contact a young member outside the product, and never use a young member's name, face or progress in anything public.
- Report any concern about a child's safety to Trust & safety and the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder straight away, the same day.

## Performance, feedback and growth

- **Goals:** each quarter you and your team lead agree three to five goals on the work board, tied to your department's SOP and the company's numbers.
- **Weekly:** your team lead reviews your work board and timesheet with you; problems are raised the week they happen, not saved for a review.
- **Quarterly review:** a written conversation in the team hub: what went well, what didn't, what is stuck, the way forward and your goals for next quarter. Your own view is asked for first.
- **Annual review:** your role, your rate or salary, and, where your arrangement includes them, any further option grants, decided by the founder on your team lead's recommendation.
- **Learning:** take Notes Academy courses that help your work during your working hours, with your team lead's agreement, and log the time. Teach what you know to the team, too.
- **Growing into a new role:** internal roles are posted in the team hub before they're advertised outside.

## Concerns, grievances, discipline and whistleblowing

### Raising a concern

1. Talk to your team lead first, in a call or in the team hub.
2. If that doesn't settle it, or it's about your team lead, write to the founder (or the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder) in the team hub, marked Grievance.
3. You get a written answer within 10 working days, and a meeting if you want one, where you may bring a colleague.

### Discipline

We deal with problems fairly and in proportion. Except for serious misconduct, the steps are: an informal word from your team lead; a written warning in the team hub, with what must change and by when; a final written warning; then dismissal with the notice in your contract. At each step you hear what we think happened and give your side before anything is decided.

**Serious misconduct** can lead to dismissal without notice. It includes: lies, theft or fraud, including logging hours you didn't work; misusing or leaking member data; harming or endangering a young member; harassment or violence; accepting bribes; and sharing confidential company information.

### Whistleblowing

If you believe something wrong is happening (fraud, a danger to members, a breach of the law or of this handbook), report it to the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder or to Legal & compliance in the team hub, even if you're not sure. You won't be punished for raising a concern honestly, even if it turns out to be mistaken.

## Leaving NotesApp

- **Notice:** what your contract says (during probation, the shorter period it states; after it, usually one month), given in writing in the team hub. The company may pay you in place of notice.
- **Handover:** in your last two weeks, hand every open task to a named colleague on the work board and write a short handover note.
- **Your last day:** your staff login and access end that day, and any company equipment comes back. Your final pay covers the hours or days you worked, and any approved expenses, on the next pay day.
- **Stock options:** unvested options end on your last day. You have 14 days to exercise vested options; after that they end. Ask Nana-HR or the Board Secretary for your figures before you go.
- **After you leave:** confidentiality continues, company information stays with us, and for the period in your contract you don't solicit our staff or approach our members and partners using what you learned here.
- **Exit conversation:** we ask every leaver what we could do better. It's optional, and it helps the next person.
