# Product & tech brief

**Product decides what NotesApp becomes: one account that opens every door for Africa's creators, professionals, organisations and learners, built for the phones and data they actually have.** Product & tech owns the why and the what; Engineering, a department of its own, owns the how, and the two work side by side.

## Principles

1. **Share, learn or earn.** A feature earns its place only if it helps a member do one of the three.
2. **A mid-range Android on slow, costly data** is the phone we design for.
3. **Naira first; commissions in the open.**
4. **Our own tools first:** improve what we have before adding something new, and never send members elsewhere for what we do.
5. **Safe for young members by design.**
6. **It isn't done until its public pages say so:** changelog, help, pricing, roadmap.

## How decisions are made

A problem written first (who, today, the cost, the number to move) → a spec in the team hub → mockups for major UI → the CEO approves → Engineering builds → launch with public pages → one week later, check the number.
