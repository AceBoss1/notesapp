## 1. Why this playbook exists

In August 2026 SMEDAN and Zoho opened a programme worth up to ₦20bn in Zoho "wallet credits" for up to 25,000 Nigerian MSMEs. The credits cover Zoho Books (accounting), Bigin (CRM) and Zoho Workplace (business email), plus training, workshops and consultations. Applicants need a valid 16-digit SMEDAN certificate ID (SUID). Businesses without one can register free on the SMEDAN portal. Press reports name the application page as smedan.gov.ng/zoho **[unverified: check the live URL]**. Reports also carry a "do not pay agents" warning.

The pattern is simple:

1. The agency has a mandate and a target group.
2. The vendor gives **credits, not cash**.
3. The agency's own ID proves eligibility.
4. Users apply only on the agency's official portal.
5. Both sides get a public win and a number to report.

The agency spends no budget. So the deal can often be signed as an MoU, not a procurement contract. That is the deal we want to repeat.

Our aim is to sign similar deals with Nigerian agencies so that their target groups (MSMEs, students, trainees, corps members, artisans, schools) adopt NotesApp products.

## 3. Target list

Priority key:
- **1** = approach in the first 30 days. Clear hook, live programme, low cost to the agency.
- **2** = approach in days 30 to 90. Good fit, but slower or needs a first win.
- **3** = long-term or relationship only. Low odds or high risk.

"Who to approach" names roles. Names of officers change often. Check the agency website on the day you write.

### 3.1 MSME and enterprise agencies

#### SMEDAN (Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency of Nigeria)
- **Who they are.** Federal agency for MSME development. Runs the MSME register that issues the 16-digit SMEDAN certificate ID (SUID).
- **Live hooks.** SMEDAN–Zoho credits (August 2026). SMEDAN–CAC free business name registration for 250,000 MSMEs (reported from late 2025 into 2026; current status **[unverified]**). SMEDAN–MTN digital capacity partnership (reported December 2025). NITDA–SMEDAN partnership to digitise MSMEs.
- **Product fit.** Business books, inventory, Notes Market, booking calendar with Paystack, Notes Academy (bookkeeping and selling online courses), Partner Network badge ("SMEDAN-registered").
- **Offer shape.** NotesApp credits for SUID holders. Example: 6 or 12 months of Business plan free, or a ₦ credit wallet usable across plans and Notes Market fees. Free "Run your business with records" course with a QR certificate. We position as **complementary to Zoho**: simpler, mobile-first, with a shop and delivery built in.
- **Value to them.** More MSMEs on the register. A second credit programme to announce. Reportable numbers (activations, records kept, sales made).
- **Who to approach.** Office of the Director-General. Director in charge of partnerships or enterprise development. Head of the MSME digital/ICT unit.
- **Priority. 1.**

#### CAC (Corporate Affairs Commission)
- **Who they are.** Federal company and business name registry.
- **Hook.** Free registration drives (with SMEDAN, and a separate reported 3,500-SME drive). CAC is also digitising its services.
- **Product fit.** Onboarding tie-in: "Registered with CAC? Get NotesApp Business tools." Business books for newly registered firms. Partner Network badge for verified registration.
- **Offer shape.** Credits for newly registered business names (verified by RC/BN number). We do **not** handle registration ourselves unless we become an accredited agent.
- **Value to them.** Shows new registrants a reason to stay formal. Supports their digital push.
- **Who to approach.** Registrar-General's office. Head of business name registration. CAC partnerships/ICT department.
- **Priority. 2.** Best done through SMEDAN first.

#### BOI (Bank of Industry)
- **Who they are.** Federal development finance institution.
- **Hooks.** BOI has called 2026 its "digital take-off year". It plans to put 15% of MSME financing towards digital and IT projects (reported). It signed an MoU with Bumpa for 200 youth-led MSMEs (October 2025). It manages the FGN/BOI MSME Intervention Fund.
- **Product fit.** Business books (clean records for loan applications), inventory, Notes Market.
- **Offer shape.** Free Business plan for BOI loan applicants and beneficiaries. A "loan-ready records" course on Notes Academy. Optional data-sharing only with the MSME's explicit consent.
- **Value to them.** Better records from borrowers. Lower default risk.
- **Who to approach.** Head of MSME/SME directorate. Head of digital or innovation. Partnerships desk.
- **Priority. 2.** The Bumpa MoU shows they sign with platforms. We must not look like a copy.

## 5. Standard deal structure

Use this for every agency. Change only the names and numbers.

1. **Credits, not cash.** NotesApp gives plan months, wallet credits or exam packs. No money goes from NotesApp to the agency or any official. No money goes from the agency to NotesApp unless a separate, lawful procurement contract exists.
2. **Headline value.** State the value as "up to ₦X in NotesApp credits". Compute it at our public list price × number of places. Say it is list-price value. Cap the total.
3. **Eligibility by the agency's own ID.** Examples: SMEDAN SUID (16 digits), NYSC state code, 3MTT fellow ID, LSETF beneficiary ID, ITF SUPA ID, CAC RC/BN number, startup label number, school/student ID issued by the state. NotesApp checks the ID against a list or API the agency provides. If the agency cannot provide a check, we use a one-time code that the agency issues.
4. **Apply only on the official portal.** Applications start on the agency's site (for example a page on the agency's .gov.ng domain) and land on a NotesApp page for that programme. We never take applications by WhatsApp, email or agents.
5. **Anti-fraud warning, on every page, message and poster:**
   > "This programme is free. Never pay anyone, including agents, to apply. NotesApp and [Agency] will never ask for payment, your bank PIN or your OTP. Apply only at [official URL]. Report fraud to [email/phone]."
6. **Time limit.** Credits must be claimed by a set date and used within a set period (for example 12 months).
7. **No cash-out.** Credits cannot be sold, transferred or swapped for cash.
8. **After the free period.** Users drop to the Free plan automatically. No surprise charges. No card needed to claim.
9. **Training included.** At least one free course with a QR certificate, plus live sessions.
10. **Reporting.** Monthly dashboard to the agency with aggregate numbers only.
11. **No exclusivity** either way.
12. **Data.** NotesApp is the data controller for its users. Personal data is not shared with the agency unless the user consents and the MoU allows it. See section 7.

## 6. One-page pitch outline

Keep it to one page. Print it. Bring it to every first meeting.

1. **Title.** "[Agency] x NotesApp: [number] [target group] online in [months], at no cost to government."
2. **The problem, in their words.** Quote their own target (for example DL4ALL 70% by 2027, 3MTT job outcomes, CBT readiness).
3. **What we give.** Credits worth up to ₦X (list price). Places: N. Products named plainly.
4. **How it works.** Four steps: register with the agency, get your ID, apply on the official portal, start using NotesApp.
5. **What it costs you.** Nothing. No procurement. An MoU and a link on your site.
6. **What you get.** Monthly numbers for your reports. A joint launch. QR certificates people can check.
7. **Safety.** NDPA 2023 compliance. Data stays in our control. No agents. No fees.
8. **Proof.** Our current users and results (real numbers only). The SMEDAN–Zoho and LSETF–Canva deals as precedents.
9. **About us.** NotesApp Technologies Ltd, Lagos. CAC number. Nigerian team. US parent for investment and support.
10. **The ask.** "Sign an MoU for a 12-month pilot. Launch in 60 days."
11. **Contact.** One named NotesApp lead.

## 7. MoU outline

Have a Nigerian lawyer draft and review. This is an outline, not legal advice.

1. **Parties.** [Agency] and NotesApp Technologies Ltd (RC number, address in Lagos). State if NotesApp Inc is a party. Keep the Nigerian company as the signing party by default.
2. **Background.** The agency's mandate and programme. NotesApp's products.
3. **Purpose and scope.** Number of beneficiaries. Products. Credit value at list price. Training. States or groups covered.
4. **Roles.**
   - Agency: share eligibility verification (list, API or codes); host the application link on its official portal; co-promote; name a focal officer.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What it looks like | What we do |
|---|---|---|
| **Procurement rules** | An official says the deal needs a tender. Or a free offer is later turned into a paid contract without due process. | Keep MoUs free and non-exclusive. Any paid work goes through the Public Procurement Act 2007 (federal) or the state procurement law. BPP revised federal thresholds in May 2025; check the current thresholds before any paid bid. Register on the BPP National Database of Contractors if we bid. |
| **Political change** | New minister, DG or commissioner drops the programme. 2027 is an election year; reports already link some 2026 programmes to election timing. | Sign with the institution, not the person. Get the MoU signed by the authorised signatory. Brief the permanent staff (directors), not only political appointees. Keep programmes small enough to survive a pause. |
| **Payment delays** | If any deal becomes paid, payment waits on budget release (for example NDE's reported Phase II delays). | Prefer credit deals. For paid deals, ask for milestone payments and do not front large costs. |
| **Fraud** | Fake "agents" charge for "registration". Fake pages copy our programme. Code reselling. | Fraud warning everywhere. Codes tied to verified IDs. One claim per ID. Rate limits. Monitor social media for fake pages. Report to the agency and, where needed, to the police or EFCC. |
| **Exam body IP and endorsement** | Using WAEC, NECO or JAMB names or past questions without rights. Implying official approval. | Licence content. Use "practice for", never "official". Legal review of all exam marketing. |
| **Data protection** | Agency wants raw student or MSME data. Breach of children's data. | Aggregate reports only. Extra care with under-18s: parental consent where required. NDPA compliance. |
| **Reputation** | Accused of using government to sell. Free users feel trapped. | No card to claim. Clear end date. Free plan stays free. |
| **Capacity** | 25,000 users sign up in a week. Support breaks. | Phased intake. Waitlist. Self-serve onboarding. |
| **Competitor overlap** | Agency already has Zoho, Canva or Bumpa deals. | Position as complementary. Focus on what they lack (shop + delivery, exam prep, QR certificates, local payments). |
| **Cost of credits** | Credits are "free" to the agency but cost us hosting, support, SMS and data. | Cap places. Model cost per user **[estimate needed]** before signing. |
