# Community Strategist brief

Nov 8, 2026 · Emmanuel Adams, Founder, CEO & CTO #NotesApp Inc.

**Communities bring whole audiences to NotesApp at once, and keep them coming back every week. The Community Strategist makes every community a success: well set up, active, safe, and growing.** The role sits in Customer care and reports to the Customer care team lead. Read it with the Customer care and trust & safety brief, the SOP: Community Strategist, and the Employee Handbook.

## Why communities matter

A church, mosque, NGO, school, alumni group, club, creator or business brings its people with it. On NotesApp they gather at their own address (/c/name), hear from their leaders in **General**, talk in **Spaces**, work in small private **Circles**, meet live in **Audio rooms** and **lives**, run **Events** with tickets, and collect **giving** (tithes, offerings, dues and donations) in naira. Each active community is hundreds of members who post, give, buy, learn and come back. That's why communities are the flagship of our Marketing Brief.

## Who you serve

- **Community owners and admins:** pastors, imams, NGO directors, head teachers, alumni secretaries, creators and business owners. Busy, often not technical, often on a phone and slow data. They want their people together and their giving safe.
- **Moderators and hosts:** the volunteers who keep Spaces calm and host lives and audio rooms.
- **Members:** the people the community exists for. You serve them through their admins.

## What you own

- **Onboarding:** every new community welcomed within 2 working days, and taken from sign-up to its first event: rules, roles, moderators, Spaces, what non-members see, and a first live, room or event.
- **Activity:** helping quiet communities get going, and planning lives, audio rooms, series and events with active hosts.
- **Safe giving, with Trust & safety:** no giving appeal is promoted before the organisation is verified; scams and look-alike appeals reported the same day.
- **The admins' voice:** what communities ask for, taken to Product in their own words.
- **The numbers:** active communities and first events, reported every month.

## What you don't do

- Settle a conflict between members, or remove members or content yourself: that's Trust & safety.
- Promise features, dates, prices or money: Product and Finance decide those.
- Sign deals or partnerships: Sales & partnerships brings organisations in, and the CEO or CFO signs.
- Move a community's work to WhatsApp or other apps: our own tools first.

## How you work with others

| Who | Together you… |
| --- | --- |
| Customer care team lead | agree your weekly priorities; hand over members' questions that aren't about communities |
| Trust & safety | check organisations before giving is promoted; send them conflicts, scams and reports |
| Sales & partnerships | take every newly signed organisation from contract to live within 7 days |
| Marketing | plan community campaigns, the challenge and community success stories |
| Product | bring admins' requests and problems, with their words and how many asked |
| Editorial | turn great community stories into journal posts on our own journals |

## Principles

- **Their community, their voice.** We advise; the owner and admins decide how their community runs, within our terms.
- **Safety first, and children first.** Young members' protections are never switched off, and any risk to a child goes to Trust & safety within the hour.
- **Giving is sacred.** People give to what they trust. A giving appeal is promoted only when the organisation is verified.
- **Follow up until it happens.** A welcome without a first event isn't onboarding.
- **Written down, in the team hub.** Every community you help has a task on the work board, and every promise has a date.

## Priorities for the first 90 days

1. The onboarding checklist for new communities, in the help centre and the team hub.
2. A monthly call with the 20 most active community admins.
3. The giving safety playbook, with Trust & safety.
4. Every community signed by Sales & partnerships live within 7 days.

## Measures

- Communities active each week: a post, a live or a room (target set each quarter with the Customer care team lead).
- New communities holding a first event within 30 days (target: 7 in 10).
- New communities welcomed within 2 working days (target: all).
- Giving appeals promoted without a verified organisation (target: none).
- Admins' satisfaction, each quarter.
