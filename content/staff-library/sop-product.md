# SOP: Product & tech

**Purpose:** decide what we build and why, so every feature helps a member share, learn or earn, works on a mid-range Android phone on slow data, and launches with its public pages. **Owner:** the CPO, or the Product & tech team lead until that role is filled. Product & tech works side by side with Engineering, a department of its own, which builds and ships what Product & tech decides.

## Routines

- **Daily:** member feedback and leads in the team hub; the work board for product tasks in flight.
- **Weekly:** the roadmap review with Engineering and the CEO: what's next, what's blocked, what's waiting on a decision.
- **Monthly:** read the numbers by door (sign-ups, activation, paying members) with Marketing and Finance; pick the problems worth solving next.
- **Quarterly:** check the public roadmap, pricing page and help centre still match the product.

## Procedures

### From idea to approved spec

1. Write the problem first: who it's for, what they do today, what it costs them, and the number that should move.
2. Check our own tools first: if NotesApp already does it, improve that instead of adding something new.
3. Draft the spec in the team hub: the member's steps, what's out of scope, the limits by plan, the young-members check and the data it collects.
4. For major UI, get phone and desktop mockups approved by the CEO before building; for Notes Life, mockups always come first.
5. The CEO approves the spec on its task; Engineering estimates and schedules it.

### Prices, limits and settings

1. Prices and commissions are changed only by the founder in Admin → Pricing; plan limits in Admin → Limits.
2. Product prepares each change with the reason and the members affected, and the help and pricing pages that read the figure live are checked after.
3. Tell Marketing and Customer care in the team hub the day a price or limit changes.

### Launch

1. Confirm the changelog entry, help article and public pages are in the release.
2. Test it on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data, signed in as a member, and as a teen where it applies.
3. Brief Customer care and Marketing in the team hub with what changed and the words to use.
4. One week after: check the number it was meant to move, and write what you learned on the task.

### Feedback

Every piece of member feedback is read; anything repeated three times or more becomes a task with the members' words quoted.

## Measures

- Features that moved the number they were built for.
- Releases launched with their public pages (target: all).

## When to escalate

- A feature that would weaken young-members rules or collect new personal data: Legal, before the spec is approved.
- A launch that isn't working on a mid-range phone: hold it, and tell the CEO.
