# Customer care and trust & safety brief

**Customer care makes every member feel looked after, and Trust & safety makes NotesApp a place people trust with their money, their words and their children.** Both are the member's voice inside the company.

## Who we serve

Readers and buyers, creators and sellers, professionals with bookings, churches and NGOs running communities, teachers and learners, students and their parents, players in Notes Life, and Enterprise partners. Most write from a phone, often on slow data, often worried about money.

## How we sound

- **Warm and plain:** "We've found the problem and here's what happens next." Never jargon, never blame.
- **Specific:** amounts in naira, dates, the next step and who takes it.
- **Honest:** say what we can and can't do; never promise a refund or a change before the team that owns it confirms.
- **In their language where we can:** Nana translates between English, Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, Yoruba and Kiswahili.

## Our first line: the help centre and Nana

Most questions should be answered before anyone writes to us. Keep the help articles right, link them in every reply, and send Product the questions Nana couldn't answer, so the next member doesn't have to ask.

## Safety principles

- **Children first:** any risk to a young member is acted on within the hour, and their protections are never switched off.
- **Fair and consistent:** the same rule for every member, written in our terms; every decision explained, every appeal reviewed by someone new.
- **Privacy:** look up a member only when the work needs it; every view of an email or phone is logged.
- **Scams are our enemy:** fake giving appeals, look-alike accounts and payment fraud are removed fast and reported to Finance.

## Priorities for the next 90 days

1. Rotas that cover big lives, Black Fridays and the challenge launch.
2. Saved replies in our voice for the top 20 questions.
3. A safety playbook for communities that collect giving.

## Measures

- First reply within one working day (target: 9 in 10).
- Safety reports about young members acted on within the hour (target: all).
- Member satisfaction from the feedback form, monthly.
