# SOP: Community Strategist

**Purpose:** help every community on NotesApp get set up well, stay active and stay safe. **Owner:** the Community Strategist, who reports to the Customer care team lead. Trust & safety owns conflicts, removals and appeals. Read with the Community Strategist brief.

**What this role never does:** remove members or content, settle conflicts between members, approve or promote a giving appeal before the organisation is verified, promise features, prices or dates, or move a community's work off NotesApp.

## Routines

- **Daily:** new communities from the last day (welcome within 2 working days); admins' messages in the team hub; reports about communities passed to Trust & safety.
- **Weekly (Monday):** review the most active and the quiet communities; plan the week's lives, audio rooms and events with active hosts; offer help to the quiet ones.
- **Weekly (Friday):** a short note to the Customer care team lead: communities welcomed, first events held, anything stuck.
- **Monthly:** the call with the 20 most active community admins; the monthly report; the top requests to Product.

## Procedures

### A new community

1. Within 2 working days, send its owner and admins a welcome in Messages, with the onboarding checklist (in the help centre) and a time for a short call if they want one.
2. Add it to the team hub work board as a task, with its address (/c/name), the owner, and the first event date once agreed.
3. Go through the checklist with the admins:
   1. **Rules:** clear community rules, in their own words, within our terms.
   2. **Roles:** at least one admin besides the owner, and moderators for busy Spaces.
   3. **Spaces:** a few to start (for example Announcements, Prayer requests, Youth), each public or members only, as they choose.
   4. **What non-members see:** whether General is open to people who haven't joined.
   5. **A first event:** a live, an audio room or an event in the next 30 days.
4. Follow up a week later. A community without a first event after 30 days gets a call.

### A community joined through Sales & partnerships

1. Sales & partnerships hands over the organisation with the signed details as a team hub task.
2. Get it live within 7 days of signing: the checklist above, plus anything agreed in the contract.
3. Tell Sales & partnerships when it's live, and add the first event date to the task.

### Giving, tithes and donations

1. Before a community's first giving appeal is promoted anywhere, ask Trust & safety to confirm the organisation is verified. Record their answer on the task.
2. Never promote, share or feature an appeal that isn't verified, however urgent it sounds.
3. A look-alike community, a fake appeal or a member reporting a scam goes to Trust & safety the same day, and Finance if money moved.
4. Questions about payouts or fees go to Finance; never quote figures yourself.

### Lives, audio rooms and events

1. Plan them with the host at least a week ahead: the title, the time, who speaks, whether it's recorded, and tickets if any.
2. Check the host knows the controls: muting, bringing people on stage, slow mode in the chat, and who to call if something goes wrong.
3. For a big live (a service, a launch, a graduation), tell Customer care and Trust & safety the day before so the rota covers it.
4. After it, ask the host what went well, and share a short summary with Marketing if it's a story worth telling.

### A quiet community

1. A community with no post, live or room for 3 weeks gets a friendly message to its admins: what's working in similar communities, and one simple next step.
2. If there's no answer in a week, try once more, then leave a note on the task. Never pressure them.

### Requests and problems

1. What admins ask for goes to Product as a team hub task in their own words, with how many communities asked.
2. Something broken goes to Engineering through Customer care's usual path, with steps, screenshots and the community's address.
3. A conflict between members, a complaint about a leader or a report of abuse goes to Trust & safety at once. Anything about a child, within the hour.

### The monthly report

To the Customer care team lead, in the team hub, by the 5th working day of the month: communities active each week, new communities welcomed and how fast, first events held within 30 days, giving appeals checked, and the top 5 requests.

## Measures

- New communities welcomed within 2 working days (target: all).
- New communities holding a first event within 30 days (target: 7 in 10).
- Communities active each week (a post, a live or a room).
- Giving appeals promoted without a verified organisation (target: none).
- Admins' satisfaction, each quarter.

## When to escalate

- Any risk to a child or young member: Trust & safety, within the hour.
- A suspected scam or fake giving appeal: Trust & safety the same day, and Finance if money moved.
- An organisation signed by Sales & partnerships not live after 7 days: the Customer care team lead.
- A request about contracts, prices or partnerships: the Customer care team lead, who takes it to the CEO or CFO.
