# SOP: Chief of Staff and executive assistants

**Purpose:** keep the leadership team's priorities, meetings and follow-ups in order, so decisions turn into action. **Owner:** the Chief of Staff, who reports to the CEO. The Senior Executive Assistant and Executive Assistants report to the Chief of Staff.

| Role | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Chief of Staff | Priorities and the leadership agenda; coordination between product, operations, finance and compliance; the 90-day plan; follow-up on every leadership decision |
| Senior Executive Assistant | The C-suite's diaries, meeting packs and minutes; board and investor paperwork with the Board Secretary; travel and visits |
| Executive Assistant | Scheduling, notes, task follow-up and filing for the people they support |

**What this office never does:** approve spending or contracts (only the CEO or the CFO do), speak for the company to press or investors unless the CEO names them, or move company work outside the team hub.

## Routines

- **Daily:** check the leadership request queue in the team hub; confirm the day's meetings have an agenda and the right people; chase any decision due that day.
- **Monday:** publish the leadership week in the team hub: the priorities, the meetings, and the decisions due. Collect each department's weekly report before the leadership meeting.
- **Friday:** send the CEO a one-page week in review: decisions made, what slipped and why, what's due next week.
- **Monthly:** the 90-day plan check-in with the C-suite; board and investor updates prepared with the CFO and Board Secretary.

## Procedures

### A request for a leadership decision

1. The request arrives as a team hub task to the Chief of Staff, with what's needed, the options and a due date. Requests without them go back to the sender.
2. The Chief of Staff checks it's complete, adds any numbers the decider needs, and puts it on the right leader's agenda.
3. The decision is written on the task, in the decider's words, the same day, and the owner of the next step is assigned.
4. The Chief of Staff follows up until the next step is done.

### Running a leadership meeting

1. The agenda is in the team hub 24 hours before, each item with an owner and the decision wanted.
2. During the meeting, an assistant takes notes in the team hub's Meetings.
3. Within 2 hours after, every decision and action is a task with an owner and a due date, and the notes are shared with those who need them.

### Board and investor paperwork

1. Drafts come from the CEO or CFO; the Senior Executive Assistant formats and files them in the team hub library.
2. Nothing is signed or sent until the CEO approves it; the Board Secretary keeps the signed copies.
3. Pay, equity, funding and financial figures are confidential: share them only with the people named on the task.

### Onboarding, department assignments and leavers (with Nana-HR)

1. When an offer is signed, open the onboarding task in the team hub for the team lead.
2. Before day one, assign the new team member to their departments in the team hub (Team access), and make sure their staff login is set up with a passkey or security key. Until we have an HR team, the Chief of Staff assigns new team members to any department, and moves existing team members between departments when the founder or their team lead asks. The Chief of Staff can't change their own departments or make anyone a super admin: only the founder does those. When we hire a head of HR, the HR office sits under the Chief of Staff and takes on this work.
3. Check the handbook and the department SOP and brief are assigned in the library.
4. Prepare the internal announcement from the template: role, start date, who they report to, how to work with them. Never include pay, equity or financial figures.
5. When someone leaves, remove their departments and access on their last day, and record it on the task.

## Measures

- Leadership decisions made by their due date (target: 9 in 10).
- Meeting actions with an owner and due date within 2 hours (target: all).
- The CEO's week in review sent every Friday.

## When to escalate

- A decision more than 3 working days late: to the CEO.
- Anything about money, contracts or legal risk: to the CEO and CFO, or Legal & compliance, the same day.
