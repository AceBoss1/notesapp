# SOP: Finance and payouts

**Purpose:** make sure every naira that moves through NotesApp reaches the right person, on time, and is recorded. **Owner:** the CFO, or the Finance team lead until that role is filled. Only Finance and super admins see payments, payouts and payroll.

## Routines

- **Daily:** payouts awaiting a transfer OTP; failed or reversed payouts; refund tasks from Customer care.
- **Weekly:** reconcile the money ledger with Paystack and Paylony; review referral and affiliate withdrawals and any jump alerts.
- **Monthly:** the payroll run; revenue by source (plans, badges, commissions, giving and ticket charges, boosts, ads, add-ons) for the leadership and the Marketing report; grants and subscriptions due.

## Procedures

### Member payouts

1. Earnings are held for the period each product sets (for example, gifts 7 days, course sales 7 days after the sale, giving a day later) and then released automatically.
2. When Paystack asks for a transfer OTP, the payout waits as Awaiting OTP in Admin → Payments. Enter the code there, never in a chat, and only one finance person handles each payout.
3. A failed payout: check the member's bank details with them through Customer care, then release it again. Never change a member's bank details on their behalf.

### Refunds

1. A refund starts as a team hub task from Customer care, with the order or booking reference.
2. Check it against the product's rule (for example, a course not started within 7 days; an Exam Prep pack within 7 days).
3. Issue it through the payment it came from, record it in the ledger, and close the task so Customer care can tell the member.

### Referral and affiliate withdrawals

1. Members withdraw cash from ₦2,000, or take NotesApp Credit with a 10% bonus.
2. A sudden jump raises an alert and a hold in Admin → Referrals: check for self-referrals and fake accounts before releasing.
3. Refunded plan payments reverse the referral earning.

### Payroll

1. On each staff member's pay day, build the month's run: monthly staff get their salary; hourly staff their approved timesheet hours × their rate.
2. Record any adjustment with a written reason; deductions only as the law requires or the person agreed in writing.
3. Pay by bank transfer, then mark each line paid, one person at a time; each writes a payroll entry in the ledger.
4. Staff bank numbers are stored encrypted; you see only the last four digits. Pay figures are confidential.

### Spending and expenses

1. Nothing is bought or committed until the CEO or the CFO approves it in the team hub.
2. Staff expense claims need prior approval and a receipt; approved claims are paid with the next payroll run.

## Measures

- Payouts released on their due date (target: 99 in 100).
- The ledger reconciled every week, with no unexplained difference.
- Payroll paid on every pay day (target: all).

## When to escalate

- Money missing, a suspected fraud or a payout to the wrong account: the CFO and the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO, the same hour.
- A payment provider problem affecting members: Engineering and Customer care, at once.
