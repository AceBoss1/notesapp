# Executive office brief

**The executive office turns the founder's decisions into action across the company, and, until we have an HR team, looks after our people.** It is the Chief of Staff and Senior Executive Assistant (one person, one role) and the Executive Assistants who work under them, for the CEO and the C-suite. When we hire a head of HR, the HR office sits inside the executive office, under the Chief of Staff.

## What it owns

- **The leadership rhythm:** priorities, the leadership agenda, decisions written down with an owner and a date, and follow-up until they're done.
- **Coordination:** between product, operations, finance and compliance, so no decision falls between departments.
- **The 90-day plan:** for growth and launch work, kept current with the C-suite.
- **Board and investor paperwork:** prepared with the CFO and the Board Secretary.
- **People, while there is no HR team:** hiring, onboarding and department assignments, with Nana-HR's help. A future head of HR takes these on and reports to the Chief of Staff.

## The people side

The Chief of Staff acts as HR until we hire one:

- **Assigns departments** in the team hub: puts new team members in their departments, and moves existing team members between departments, when the founder or their team lead asks. The Chief of Staff can't change their own departments or make anyone a super admin; only the founder does those.
- **Runs hiring and onboarding:** roles posted once the founder approves them; interviews booked; offers drafted from templates for Legal and the CEO; the first-week checklist; the internal announcement.
- **Keeps people records right:** who is in which department, who leads it, who has left (and that their access ended that day).
- **Is the first stop for people questions** Nana-HR can't answer, after the person's team lead.

## Principles

- **Written down, in the team hub.** A decision that isn't on a task didn't happen.
- **No decision without an owner and a date.**
- **Confidential by default:** pay, equity, funding and financial figures stay with the people named.
- **The office coordinates; it doesn't approve.** Spending and contracts are for the CEO or CFO.
