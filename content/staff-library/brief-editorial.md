# Editorial brief

**Editorial keeps what people learn and read on NotesApp worth trusting: our own journals, the Academy's courses, Naija Exam Prep's questions and the topics that organise them all.** Members own their words; Editorial looks after ours and the shelves they sit on.

## What Editorial owns

| Area | What we do | In the team hub |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NotesApp's own journals (@notesapp, @na-notesapp) | Announcements, how-tos and stories from members, written in our house style and published on a regular rhythm | Admin → Journals |
| Journals and notes | With Trust & safety, edit our own posts and unpublish members' posts only when they break the rules; we never rewrite a member's work | Admin → Journals, Admin → Notes |
| Notes Academy | Check courses and certificates for quality and accuracy, and help teachers fix what falls short before it reaches learners | Admin → Academy |
| Naija Exam Prep | Publish the WAEC and NECO questions that subject reviewers approve, manage the reviewers, and import new subjects | Admin → Naija Exam Prep |
| Topics | Keep the topics and subtopics members choose clear, without duplicates, and in step with what members write about | Admin → Topics |

## Principles

- **Members' voices first:** we fix our own content and remove what breaks the rules; we don't rewrite members' work.
- **Checked twice:** every exam answer is approved by a subject reviewer and then by an editor before it goes live; a reported error is fixed within 7 days.
- **Plain words, our house style:** NotesApp is one word (#NotesApp as a hashtag), naira as ₦, short sentences, and no claim we can't show.
- **Africa's languages:** we write for readers in English, Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa, Yoruba and Kiswahili, and use Nana's translations with a human check.
- **Young readers protected:** content for teen and kids modes is checked with Trust & safety before it's shown.

## Working with others

Editorial sits under the COO with Customer care and Trust & safety. It works with **Trust & safety** on removals, **Legal & compliance** on takedowns and rights, **Marketing** on stories and campaigns, and **Product & tech** on topics and how content is shown.
