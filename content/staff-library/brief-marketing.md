# #NotesApp Marketing Brief

Oct 1, 2026 · Emmanuel Adams, Founder, CEO & CTO #NotesApp Inc.

## How to use this brief

This is the single guide for everyone who markets #NotesApp: the CMO, the marketing team, the user-acquisition lead, and every person who joins those teams later. Read it in your first week, and come back to it before you plan a campaign, write a post, brief an agency or talk to a partner.

**In one paragraph:** #NotesApp is one account for Nigerians to write, teach, sell, book, learn and play. A creator publishes journals and media, goes live, takes paid sessions, runs a store and a course, and gets paid in naira. A learner practises for JAMB, WAEC and NECO in Naija Exam Prep. A player lives a second life in Notes Life. Churches, mosques, foundations and NGOs run their whole community on it: a group like Facebook's, live services, events and giving. Businesses and organisations get their own branded site, and Enterprise partners connect through our API. Marketing's job is to bring in the right people for each door, show them that the other doors exist, and keep them earning, learning and coming back.

- **Who owns it:** the CMO. Anyone can comment; the CMO accepts changes.
- **How it stays current:** every release that changes what we sell or how we talk about it updates this brief in the same week, the same way the product team updates the changelog. Every change to this brief is announced in the team hub.
- **What wins when things disagree:** the live product and the public pages (pricing, help centre, changelog) win over this brief. Fix the brief.
- **Where the numbers go:** the measurement section at the end. A claim with no number behind it does not go out.

## Who we are and where we're going

**Vision: Africa's knowledge, shared, valued and rewarded.** We are building one of Africa's largest platforms, starting in Nigeria, and marketing is how it gets there.

**Mission:** #NotesApp gives Africa's creators, teachers, professionals and businesses one home to share what they know, teach it, sell it and get paid for it, in their own currency and, in time, their own language.

The mission has five pillars. Every campaign should serve at least one, and say which.

| Pillar | What it means for members | What marketing proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Share | Journals, communities, messages and calls take what people know to the people who need it | Reach: readers, followers, listeners |
| Learn | Notes Academy, Naija Exam Prep and certificates anyone can check turn knowledge into qualifications | Outcomes: scores, certificates, jobs |
| Earn | Bookings, subscriptions, gifts, Notes Market and course sales pay out in naira, with commissions in the open | Money: real payouts to real members |
| Belong | Everyone is welcome as family: readers become buyers, learners become teachers, customers become creators | Movement between roles |
| Include | Phone-first, light on data, local payments, African languages: Nana translates between English, Pidgin, Igbo, Hausa and Yoruba in text, and into Kiswahili in audio | Access: works on a mid-range Android on a slow connection |

**Our values:** members first; knowledge earns; trust and safety; built for Africa; open by default (our prices, commissions, status and changelog are public).

**How we make money.** Reading, commenting, booking and buying are free. Publishers start on the free Basic plan and move up to Pro, Business or Enterprise as they grow. We earn recurring monthly revenue from two sources, plan subscriptions and verification badges (the maroon ✔ at ₦999 a month on Free and Pro plans, included on Business and Enterprise; the gold ✔ at ₦1,999 a month for a person or ₦2,999 for an organisation, on every plan). On top of that we earn a commission on what members earn (sessions, journal subscriptions, gifts, store and course sales), flat charges on giving and tickets, paid add-ons, boosts and advertising.

**Where we're going.** Our plan takes NotesApp towards 100,000, then 1 million, then 20 million daily members. Nigeria has over 100 million internet users, so 20 million daily members would make us one of Africa's largest platforms. Marketing's targets in this brief are set against those milestones, and the platform is built to grow with the members we bring.

Two product commitments are also marketing promises, and we may say them plainly:

- **Built for Nigeria's phones and data.** Every feature has to work on a mid-range Android phone on a slow, costly connection.
- **Your data is protected.** Backups, recovery and staff access controls are built in, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We never claim more than that (no "unhackable", no "bank-grade").

## Flagship campaign: #1MillionNairaNotesAppChallenge and NotesApp Live

The challenge is our main way to bring creators, and their audiences, onto #NotesApp: up to ₦10,000,000 a month, ₦1,000,000 each for the first 10 influencers who qualify. It opens the day NotesApp Live is ready for big rooms, and not before. Until then, the page says "Coming soon" and nothing can be claimed.

### How it works (as published on /challenge)

1. **Reach the bar:** 100,000 views and 10,000 followers, and the 10,000 must be new: people who joined #NotesApp through the influencer after they enrolled.
2. **Go live** on #NotesApp for that audience.
3. **Reach 2,000 in one live:** 2,000 registered members in a single live, each for at least 5 minutes. Separate lives are never added together.
4. **The giveaway opens** for that live automatically.

| Share of each ₦1,000,000 | Goes to | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ₦500,000 | The influencer | Cash, withdrawable to their bank after an identity check (NIN and face match) and any tax that applies |
| ₦250,000 | The influencer | NotesApp Credit, to spend on anything on the platform |
| ₦250,000 | Their followers | Bonus Credits of ₦5,000 to ₦25,000 each, for followers who stayed 5+ minutes, chosen by the influencer from the most active; plus a month of the maroon ✔. Bonus Credits pay only for calls, banner ads, boosts and badges, and can't be cashed out |

Ground rules we repeat in every brief: every view, follower and live member is counted and checked; bots, fake accounts and self-viewing disqualify the account; each influencer wins once; every prize has a 30-day claim window; amounts and conditions may change before launch.

### What NotesApp Live changes, and how we adjust the challenge

A "live" on #NotesApp is a Community audio room in Live mode: sound always on, the host's camera on and off within a video-minutes budget, a poster while video is off, Audio only for listeners on slow data, and Book a session pinned at the top. That design keeps lives cheap for listeners and for us, and it changes how we talk about the challenge.

| Adjustment | Why | What changes in our copy and plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Count people **in** the live, not people **watching** | Video is optional and budgeted; most minutes of a live are audio with a poster. The room service counts each signed-in member's time in the room, whatever they watch | Say "2,000 members in one live for 5+ minutes", never "2,000 viewers". - **Visibility:** store and post boosts and banner ads are our paid levers for sellers who want to stand out each Friday, most of all on the main day.
- **The rule:** real discounts only. A "before" price must be the price the item really sold at; no raised prices to fake a deal.

## Guardrails

These are not suggestions. Anything that breaks one of them is pulled, whoever made it.

**Claims**

- Only market what is live today; check the changelog and /status first. "Coming soon" items say so in the same sentence.
- Every number needs a source in our own dashboards or a public source we can link. No "Nigeria's biggest", "#1", "most secure" or "unhackable".
- No promised earnings, no income ranges, no "get rich". Earnings stories need the member's written consent and our check that the figures are real.
- No comparisons that name a competitor without Legal's review.

**Money**

- Real money is naira (₦); Notes Life money is N$, bought with real money and never cashed out. Never suggest that playing Notes Life earns real money.
- Challenge prizes: "up to", the conditions, the identity check and "amounts and conditions may change before launch" travel with every prize mention until the final rules are confirmed.
- Commission and holds are stated wherever earning is promoted.

**Young members**

- No ads, offers or newsletters to students in teen or kids mode; anything about paid packs goes to the parent.
- Exam Prep boards show nicknames only; never show a teen's name, face or progress in marketing.
- Kids (under 13) have accounts only through a parent. Nothing collected while someone is young is reused for adult marketing without their own consent at 18.
- Influencers in the challenge must be 18 or over.

**Privacy and consent**

- We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023: consent before marketing email, an unsubscribe in every message (announcements and the digest already carry one), and only the data we need.
- Never upload member lists to ad platforms without Legal's sign-off and a lawful basis.
- Recorded lives: everyone is told, speakers agree before their mic goes on. Use a clip from a replay only if the host published it and everyone in the clip agreed.

**Brand**

- Files only from the /brand logo pack. Seasonal marks on our own channels only, for their window only.
- Nobody is called a partner, ambassador or sponsor without a written agreement.
