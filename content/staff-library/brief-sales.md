# Sales and partnerships brief

**Sales and partnerships signs the organisations that bring whole audiences to NotesApp, and gets them live and growing.** One church, school or NGO can bring thousands of members; one Enterprise partner can bring a whole market.

## Who we sell to, and what we offer

| Who | What they get | The line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Churches and mosques | A community with live services, events, tithes and offerings with receipts, no commission on giving | "Your services live and your people together all week, in one place under your name." |
| Foundations and NGOs | A community, appeals and events, giving with receipts, the organisation gold ✔ | "Your community, your appeals and your events in one place." |
| Schools and PTAs | Naija Exam Prep for students, Community for parents, Notes Academy for staff | "Practice that explains every answer, for the whole school." |
| Associations and clubs | A community with paid events and dues | "Everything your Facebook group does, plus meetings, tickets and giving." |
| Businesses and sellers | Store, booking, business books, logistics, Business plan | "Your shop, your bookings and your profit in one app." |
| Enterprise and API partners | The API, webhooks, Console, custom domains, Enterprise Courses and the Academy API | "Plug NotesApp into your business." |
| Notes Life partner brands | A real outlet, jobs and billboards in the game | "Meet young Nigerians where they play." |

## Principles

- **Sell what's live.** Demo on a phone; never promise a feature that isn't there.
- **Prices from /pricing, on the day.** Any special rate is agreed by the CEO or CFO.
- **Written agreements only.** Nobody is a partner, sponsor or ambassador without one.
- **Live within a week of signing,** with Customer care.
- **Every faith served the same way.**
