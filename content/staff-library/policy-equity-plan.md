**NotesApp Inc. 2026 Equity Incentive Plan**

*Draft v1. Adopted by the Board and approved by stockholders under the five-director consent (draft 1.1/3.1).*

**1. Purpose**

The Plan lets NotesApp Inc. (the “Company”) and its subsidiaries attract and keep employees, directors and individual service providers by offering them a share in the Company's success.

**2. Administration**

The Board runs the Plan. It may delegate to a committee, and to officers for grants within the limits in the Board consent, as DGCL §157(c) allows. The Board's decisions are final. No officer may grant to himself or herself or to a related person. No grant is made at the “sole discretion” of any single officer.

**3. Shares reserved**

- **Plan reserve:** 15% of the Company's fully diluted capital on the Effective Date (1,500,000 shares) of Common Stock.

- **FEBO reserve:** the unsold part of the angel block (5% per US\$10,000, up to 15%). It carries no votes and is not counted for voting while unallocated, and may be granted from only by the Board or within a §157(c) delegation.

- Shares from options that end, lapse or are cancelled return to the reserve they came from.

**4. Who may receive awards**

Employees, directors and individual consultants who provide services to the Company or a subsidiary. Awards are not made to people who provide no services; the Plan does not pay “network distributions”.

**5. Options**

- **Exercise price** not less than fair market value on the grant date, set by the Board with an independent valuation or other method it records.

- **Term** up to ten years. **Vesting** as in each grant, subject to continued service. A grant may include time and milestone vesting. Milestones are the user gates in the grant (Seed 1,000,000; Series A 10,000,000; Series B 50,000,000, cross-country; Series C 200,000,000, cross-continent), counted as Verified Registered Users and verified independently of the participant by the CFO and CPO, plus a Cumulative Platform Revenue milestone (net of refunds and gateway fees, excluding pass-through). Series C is cross-continent (Africa, South America and Asia).

- **Sunset:** a milestone not met by the date in the grant (the fifth anniversary of the grant date) lapses.

- **Payment** in cash or another way the Board approves. The Board sets any withholding.

**6. When service ends**

- Unvested awards end on the date service ends for any reason, including removal by a Board written resolution, and return to the reserve.

- Only options vested on that date remain. They may be exercised only in the 14 days after service ends. Awards not exercised then end.

- Before a Series A financing, vested options may be exercised during the 14 days before closing, after at least 14 days' notice by email and a Team Hub bell notice.

**7. Sale or listing**

On a sale of the Company or a listing, unvested awards end and return to the pool. Vested options may be exercised in the 14 days before closing. No one may claim unvested awards.

**8. Voting after exercise**

Shares from exercise are subject to the Company's Voting Agreement, signed on exercise. The Plan creates no irrevocable proxy. \[Counsel: §212(e).\]

**9. Adjustments, amendment, term**

The Board adjusts shares and prices for splits, mergers and similar events. The Board may amend or end the Plan, but not so as to cut an existing award without the holder's consent. The Plan ends ten years after adoption.

**10. Taxes and non-US participants**

Participants bear their own taxes. The Board may adopt sub-plans for participants in other countries, including Nigeria, to meet local law. \[Nigerian counsel: tax, exchange control and securities.\]

**11. Law**

The Plan is governed by Delaware law.
