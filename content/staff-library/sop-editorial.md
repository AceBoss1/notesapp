# SOP: Editorial

**Purpose:** keep what people learn and read on NotesApp accurate and worth trusting: our own journals, Notes Academy, Naija Exam Prep and the topics. **Owner:** the Editorial team lead, under the COO.

## Routines

- **Daily:** reported journal posts and notes (with Trust & safety); Exam Prep questions approved by reviewers and waiting to publish; reported question errors.
- **Weekly:** at least one post on @notesapp; a topics check for duplicates and members' requests.
- **Monthly:** questions published by subject, the courses checked, and the report to the COO.

## Procedures

### Publishing on our own journals

1. Draft it as a team hub item with the draft linked, in house style: NotesApp as one word (#NotesApp as a hashtag), naira as ₦, short sentences.
2. A second person reads it; every price, date and feature is checked against the live site (/pricing, the changelog). No claim we can't show.
3. Publish on @notesapp or @na-notesapp, share it to Moments, and tell Marketing so it can go in campaigns and the weekly digest.

### Naija Exam Prep questions

1. Import a subject's questions in Admin → Naija Exam Prep.
2. Subject reviewers check each question, its answer and its explanation in the review queue; a question sent back is fixed and returns to the queue.
3. An editor publishes only questions a reviewer approved. Nothing is published unreviewed.
4. A reported error: checked within 2 working days, then fixed and republished, or the member is told why the answer stands, within 7 days.

### Notes Academy courses

1. Check a new or changed course for accurate lessons, honest claims in its title and description, and a certificate that matches what it teaches.
2. Write to the teacher with what to fix; we never edit a teacher's lessons ourselves.
3. Copied or misleading courses go to Trust & safety, and to Legal & compliance if someone's rights are involved.

### Members' journals and notes

We don't rewrite members' work. A post is unpublished or removed only with Trust & safety, under the terms, and the member is told why and how to appeal (Customer care SOP, "A report").

### Topics

Merge duplicates and add the subtopics members ask for. A change that alters how content is shown or found is agreed with Product & tech first; note every change in the team hub.

## Measures

- Exam Prep questions reviewed and published each month.
- Reported question errors fixed within 7 days (target: all).
- Posts on NotesApp's own journals each week (target: at least one).

## When to escalate

- A copyright, defamation or impersonation complaint: Legal & compliance, the same day.
- Content that may harm a young member: Trust & safety and the Chief of Staff, within the hour.
- A wrong answer in a widely used Exam Prep pack: the COO the same day, with a correction published.
