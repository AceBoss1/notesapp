# SOP: Marketing and UA

**Purpose:** bring the right people through each NotesApp door, show them the next door, and grow the naira members earn each month. **Owner:** the CMO/the User Acquisition Lead. The #NotesApp Marketing Brief is the strategy; this SOP is how the work is done.

## Routines

- **Daily:** check the changelog for releases; post and reply on our channels; watch creator lives in the challenge.
- **Monday:** the weekly report in the team hub (naira earned by members, daily active members, sign-ups and activation by door, the challenge and community numbers), and the week's tasks assigned.
- **Monthly:** the numbers review, budget changes and an update to the Marketing Brief, announced in the team hub.

## Procedures

### Launching a release

1. Read the changelog entry and the help article.
2. Test the feature on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data.
3. Write the post in our voice: short, plain, specific, in naira, linking to naapp.tech only.
4. Post it, then tell Customer care in the team hub what went out.

### Running a campaign

1. Write a one-page plan as a team hub task: the door, the audience, the message, the channels, the budget, the campaign code or referral link, and the number it should move.
2. Get the CMO's approval, and the CEO's or CFO's for any spending, before you commit anything.
3. Check the guardrails: only what's live, prices checked against /pricing on the day, no promised earnings, no ads to teens, brand rules followed.
4. Launch, then report the result against its number within 2 weeks of the end.

### Bringing on a creator or influencer

1. Shortlist by audience fit; check they're 18 or over.
2. Use the written agreement template; Legal reviews it and the CEO or CFO approves it. Being in a campaign doesn't make anyone a NotesApp partner or ambassador.
3. Give them the host guide, the brand rules and their own referral link.
4. Track their counted figures in their dashboard, never from screenshots.

### Onboarding a community (church, mosque, NGO, association)

1. Find the leader and the volunteer who will run it; demo on a phone.
2. Set up in one week: the community at naapp.tech/c/communityname, Spaces, Circles, giving funds, the first event and the first live.
3. Launch to their members with one link and a QR code.
4. Follow up at 30 days: members, lives, giving, and whether Business and the organisation gold ✔ fit them.

### Black Fridays (November)

Every Friday in November is a Black Friday, building to the main day on the last Friday. Brief sellers and professionals on discount codes, coupons and session offers two weeks ahead; real discounts only.

## Measures

Naira earned by members each month; daily active members; activation by door; recurring revenue from plans and badges; challenge and community numbers. Targets are set by the CMO and written in the Marketing Brief.

## When to escalate

- Anything about money, contracts or young members: Legal and the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO or CFO before it goes out.
- A post that went out wrong: take it down, then tell the CMO the same hour.
