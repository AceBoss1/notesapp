# Finance brief

**Finance makes sure every naira that moves through NotesApp reaches the right person on time, is recorded, and helps the company grow.** Members' money is held in trust; our money funds the mission.

## How money flows

1. A buyer, giver or subscriber pays through our payment partners (Paystack for cards and subscriptions; Paylony for bank transfers and CardTap), or with NotesApp Credit.
2. The money is held for the period each product sets: until a session happens, an order arrives, or the hold for gifts and course sales ends.
3. Our commission or flat charge is taken, at the rates published on /pricing.
4. The rest is paid out to the member's bank automatically; Finance clears any transfer waiting for an OTP and any that fail.

## Principles

- **Members' money first:** payouts are never delayed to suit our cash flow.
- **Approved before spent:** only the CEO or CFO commits company money.
- **Every naira recorded:** the ledger reconciled every week with the payment partners.
- **Confidential:** pay, equity and financial figures go only to the people who need them.

## Payroll and people

Staff are paid on their pay day: a monthly salary, or approved Time Track hours × their rate. Options come from NotesApp Inc. under the 2026 Equity Incentive Plan and aren't part of payroll. Deductions only as the law requires or the person agrees in writing.

## Funding

Finance prepares the numbers every application needs (members in Nigeria and Kenya, publishers, paid transactions in the last 90 days), taken from the team hub on the day and never a figure we can't show: the pitch.tech competition, the .tech No Excuses Fund, accelerators and the Pre-Seed round.
