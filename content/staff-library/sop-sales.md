# SOP: Sales and partnerships

**Purpose:** sign the organisations and partners that bring whole audiences to NotesApp, and get them live and growing. **Owner:** the Growth & partnerships team lead.

| Who | What we sell them | Where they start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Churches, mosques, foundations, NGOs, associations | A community with live services, events and giving; Business for their own site and domain; the organisation gold ✔ | naapp.tech/c/communityname |
| Schools and parent-teacher associations | Naija Exam Prep for their students; Community for parents; Notes Academy for staff training | Naija Exam Prep and Community |
| Businesses and sellers | Store, booking, business books, logistics; Business plan | A free trial of Pro or Business |
| Enterprise and API partners | The API, webhooks, Console, custom domains, Enterprise Courses and the Academy API | /docs and /organisations |
| Notes Life partner brands | A real outlet, jobs and billboards in the game | Notes Life |

## Routines

- **Daily:** new leads in the team hub; follow-ups due.
- **Weekly:** the pipeline review with the CMO: each lead's stage, next step and date.
- **Monthly:** a 30-day check-in with every partner that went live.

## Procedures

### From lead to live

1. **Lead:** log it in the team hub with the organisation, the decision maker, their size and what they need.
2. **Demo:** on a phone, showing exactly what they'd use; never promise a feature that isn't live.
3. **Proposal:** the plan, prices from /pricing on the day, and any Enterprise rate as a draft for the CEO or CFO.
4. **Agreement:** from the template, reviewed by Legal, approved and signed only by the CEO or the CFO. No partner, sponsor or ambassador title without it.
5. **Set-up:** within a week of signing, with Customer care: accounts, community or site, giving funds, first event, API keys for Enterprise.
6. **Launch:** one link and a QR code to their members or customers.
7. **Grow:** the 30-day check-in; upgrades, add-ons and introductions to similar organisations.

### Enterprise and API partners

1. API access is switched on per account by the team, on the Enterprise plan only; the partner creates keys in their own Console.
2. Share the public docs at /docs; questions go to our Discord or a team hub task for Engineering.
3. Custom rates are agreed by the CEO or CFO and recorded on the account.

## Measures

- Organisations live within 7 days of signing (target: 9 in 10).
- Members brought in by each organisation in its first 30 days.
- Recurring revenue from organisations and Enterprise accounts.

## When to escalate

- Any non-standard price, term or exclusivity: the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO or CFO before it's offered.
- A partner that wants to collect money from children or teens: Legal, before anything is agreed.
