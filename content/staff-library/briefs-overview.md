## How the briefs fit together

Every department has a brief: why it exists, what it owns, what it believes, and what it must achieve. Marketing has the #NotesApp Marketing Brief and Engineering has the Engineering Guide; these are the briefs for every other department. Read your department's brief with the Employee Handbook (how we work as a company) and your SOP manual (how your department does the job, step by step).

| Department | Its brief | Its SOP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive office | Executive office tab | Chief of Staff and EAs |
| Legal & compliance | Legal and compliance tab | Legal and compliance |
| Product & tech | Product & tech tab | Product & tech |
| Engineering | The Engineering Guide | Engineering |
| Growth & partnerships: marketing | The Marketing Brief | Marketing and UA |
| Growth & partnerships: sales | Sales and partnerships tab | Sales and partnerships |
| Customer care and Trust & safety | Customer care and safety tab | Customer care and safety |
| Finance | Finance tab | Finance and payouts |

**What every brief shares:** the vision (Africa's knowledge, shared, valued and rewarded), the mission, our values, the north star (naira earned by members each month), and our own tools first. Each brief is owned by its department's lead, reviewed every quarter, and lives in the team hub library once approved.
