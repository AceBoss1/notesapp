# SOP: Legal and compliance

**Purpose:** keep NotesApp, its members and its staff on the right side of the law: data, young members, money, contracts and content. **Owner:** the Chief Compliance Officer/the Chief Legal Officer, or the Legal/compliance team lead until that role is filled. Outside counsel (Nigerian, and Delaware for NotesApp Inc.) is engaged only by the CEO.

## Routines

- **Daily:** the queue of data and legal requests, takedown reports and contract reviews in the team hub.
- **Weekly:** check new policies, templates and SOP changes in the library; review anything Trust & safety escalated.
- **Monthly:** review the staff access list with the founder (people who left removed, departments still right); review the record of the personal data we hold.
- **Quarterly:** check the terms, privacy policy and young-members rules still match the product; run a breach drill with Engineering.

## Procedures

### A data request from a member (see, correct, delete, export)

1. Customer care passes it to Legal in the team hub the day it arrives.
2. Confirm the person is the account holder before anything is shared.
3. Do what the law requires, using the product's own export and deletion tools where they fit, and answer the member in writing within the legal time limit.
4. Record the request, what was done and when, on the task.

### A request from the police, a court, a lawyer or a regulator

1. Nobody else answers it. Legal checks it's genuine and lawful, and asks for it in writing if it isn't already.
2. Share only what the law requires, through a member look-up with a logged reason.
3. The CEO is told the same day; a record is kept of what was shared and why.

### A suspected data breach

1. Within the hour: confirm what happened with Engineering and the person who reported it; stop it getting worse.
2. Within 24 hours: decide, with the CEO, whether it puts members at risk.
3. Within 72 hours of learning of it: tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if it does, and tell the members affected.
4. Write up what happened and what changes, and update the SOPs it touches.

### A takedown or content complaint (copyright, defamation, impersonation)

1. Check the complaint identifies the content and the complainant's right.
2. With Trust & safety, decide: remove, restrict or keep. Tell the member what was done and how to appeal.
3. Record the decision and the reason.

### Contracts, offers and policies

1. Every contract, offer letter, option grant, partner agreement and policy starts from a template in the team hub library.
2. Legal reviews it and records any changes from the template; outside counsel reviews anything new or unusual.
3. Only the CEO or the CFO approves and signs; the Board Secretary keeps signed corporate documents.
4. Option grants go through the Board of NotesApp Inc., under the 2026 Equity Incentive Plan.

### Young members

Check every new feature, campaign and partner deal against the young-members rules before launch: no ads or offers to teens, a parent's consent for children, nickname-only public names, nothing collected while young reused for adult marketing without fresh consent.

## Measures

- Data and legal requests answered within the legal time limit (target: all).
- Breach decisions within 24 hours of a report (target: all).
- Contracts reviewed within 5 working days.

## When to escalate

- Any breach, regulator contact or court order: the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO, the same day.
- Any risk to a child: the Chief of Staff, who passes it to the founder/CEO and Trust & safety, the same hour.
