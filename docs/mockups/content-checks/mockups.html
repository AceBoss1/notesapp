🛡️ AI content checks: mockups

Every photo, video frame and text that people outside a private chat can see is checked by AI before it is published: journals, community posts and comments, Moments, Media, shop items, events and profiles. The same rules apply to public, followers-only and subscriber-only posts. Private 1:1 and group messages are not read. Child sexual abuse material is a permanent ban and is reported.

A community is closed only when its owner or moderators keep posting or approving rule-breaking content. Members are warned first, and the owner can appeal.

4 · Strikes and appealsThree strikes in 90 days suspends posting for 14 days (both figures set in Admin). A fourth after that is a ban. Every strike shows what and when, and every one can be appealed. Teens' posts get the same check with stricter thresholds.
