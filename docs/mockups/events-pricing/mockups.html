Booking

Events, meetings and sessions

Three ways for people to book you, all on your profile, your calendar and Discover, paid in naira through Paystack or NotesApp Credit, with reminders 24 hours and 1 hour before.

🎟️

Events

One-to-many at a set time: launches, services, classes, meetups, concerts. In person, online or both.

  • Free, paid tickets (Regular, VIP…) or approval to join
  • Unlimited guests; QR tickets and check-in
  • Repeats weekly or monthly
Free events cost nothing. Tickets: ₦10 up to ₦5,000, ₦25 up to ₦50,000, ₦50 above, per ticket, plus the card charge at cost. No commission, on every plan.
Create an event
👥

Meetings

A small group at a set time: office hours, workshops, cohort calls, group coaching. New.

  • Seats (2 to 50), free or a price per seat
  • On #NotesApp video, or your Zoom / Meet link
  • Weekly or one-off; your cancellation policy
Free meetings cost nothing. Paid seats: your plan's commission, the same as sessions: Free Basic 20%, Pro 15%, Business 8%, Enterprise from 6%.
Set up a meeting
🗓️

Sessions

One-to-one, booked from your open times: coaching, consulting, therapy, tutoring, legal advice.

  • Your price (₦5,000–₦500,000) and length
  • Your weekly availability, reschedule and cancel
  • Paid out after the session
Your plan's commission: Free Basic 20%, Pro 15%, Business 8%, Enterprise from 6%. Try Pro or Business free for 15 days and see what you'd keep.
