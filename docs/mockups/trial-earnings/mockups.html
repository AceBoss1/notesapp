The end of a free trial: what you kept

Sample screens for the owner (24 Nov 2026). The pricing page doesn't do the commission maths up front (owner's choice). Instead, once someone tries Pro or Business free for 15 days, #NotesApp works it out from their real earnings in the trial: what they kept on the plan, next to what they'd have kept on their own plan. When the plan doesn't pay yet, the card says so. All figures here are sample figures at the default rates in code (Free Basic 35% and Pro 25% on sessions, subscriptions and gifts; ad share 0% and 25%); the real card uses the live rates in Admin → Pricing.

2. When it paidOn the trial's last day (and in an email): what they kept on Pro, next to what they'd have kept on Free Basic, from the same payments. Then the plan's price and the monthly pace. The choice is theirs, with both buttons the same size.

3. When it didn't pay (yet)We say so, and tell them the point where it would. Then, when a Free Basic member's last 30 days pass that point, one message: "Pro would have kept you ₦X more last month." That's the only nudge, and it's backed by their own numbers.
