📅 My Calendar: mockups

Everything you've booked, bought or been invited to on #NotesApp, in one calendar, in the same look as the booking calendar. It syncs to your phone's own calendar (iPhone, Android or Google, Outlook) through a private link that your calendar app keeps up to date. In the installed #NotesApp app, it can also add events straight to the phone's default calendar.
