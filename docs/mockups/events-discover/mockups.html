#NACreate

What are you setting up?

🎟️
An event

One-to-many: a launch, a service, a class, a meetup. Free, paid tickets or approval to join. Unlimited guests.

Tickets: ₦10–₦50 each, no commission
👥
A meeting

A small group at a set time: office hours, a workshop, a cohort call. Seats, a price per seat or free, on #NotesApp video or your Zoom/Meet link.

Paid seats: your plan's commission
🗓️
Sessions

One-to-one, booked from your open times: coaching, consulting, therapy, tutoring. Your price and length.

Your plan's commission (Free Basic 20%)

Each shows on your calendar and in Discover (unless it's private), with reminders by email, bell and phone notification.
