🎬 Moments: tidy up and captions

Two helpers for video moments and voice-overs. Trim the pauses takes the silences out of a recording, so one take sounds like a clean edit. Captions writes what you said and shows each word as you say it, so people can follow with the sound off. Both are optional, and you check everything before you share.

2 · Check the trimThe pauses show grey on the sound wave. Play it to hear the result, choose how tight the cut is, and keep any pause that matters. Nothing is deleted: the full video is still uploaded once, and the moment simply skips the grey parts when it plays.

Good to know
✂️ Trimming happens on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
💬 For captions, only the sound is sent to be written down. It isn't kept afterwards.
🗣 English first. Pidgin works but makes more mistakes.
🎵 A moment with a song and no speech has no captions.
Captions are checked like any other words on #NotesApp before a moment is shared.

6 · Settings and limitsYour choices are remembered. Trimming is free for everyone and runs on the device. Captions come with every video moment and voice-over your plan already allows. Captions go through the same content checks as a moment's text.
