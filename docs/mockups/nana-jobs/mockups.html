🧭 Nana Jobs: mockups

A new tab in /jobs. You connect your own AI. Nana then gathers jobs from Notes Jobs and from public job boards, filters out the ones that don't fit, scores the rest against what you've actually done, and gives you a short ranked list. Each job comes with why it fits and the strongest reason you might not get it. It finds and ranks. It never applies for you.

The key is checked with a free call, then kept encrypted. Only its last 4 characters are ever shown. A paid AI is never picked unless you choose it. Remove it any time.

2 · Bring your own AIThis adds Gemini, Groq and OpenAI-compatible APIs to the "connect your own key" we already have, which today is Claude only and is used by Nana. A key connected once works across Nana. If the AI is out of credit or slow, the list falls back to the rough ranking and shows the real error. It is never quietly empty.

5 · The shortlistFive to fifteen roles, each with a 0–100 score (domain, stack, seniority, location and the quality of the posting), why it fits, and the strongest reason you might not get it. Dead links are opened and removed before you see them. Notes Jobs roles keep the "Apply on #NotesApp" button. Everything else opens the firm's own page.

Why there's no "auto-apply": a bot that applies everywhere gets you blocked and wastes good chances. Nana finds the jobs and helps you write. You press Apply.

6 · Daily run and alertsIt runs every morning, plus on demand. The bell is on and email is off by default. The run shows how 2,110 openings became 12. The number of jobs read by AI each day is the member's own setting, because it's their AI and their bill. A cover-note helper uses only the "shipped" list, so Nana never invents experience.
