2 · Book and payRooms are held while the guest pays and released if unpaid. Only a payment the provider confirms can mark a booking paid; staff can't flip it by hand. A transfer that lands after the hold ended is kept and flagged for staff to rebook or refund. Cancellation rules come from the hotel's own policy.
