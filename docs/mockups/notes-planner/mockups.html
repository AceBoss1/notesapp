🗓 Notes Planner: mockups

A personal planner built on My Calendar. Nana interviews you, one question at a time, then writes your next 12 months like a product launch: one goal, three numbers, a project for every month, a few moves for every day and a Sunday review. It all lands in My Calendar beside your bookings, stays and tickets. Private to you, like My Cycle.

2 · Nana's interviewOne question at a time, in four parts: where you are, where it goes if nothing changes, what you want, then a check. When an answer is vague, Nana asks for an example, a number or a date. She gives no advice until the plan. "Go gentler" softens the questions; any question can be skipped.

7 · Sunday reviewA bell on Sunday evening opens three questions and the three numbers. If she logs one of the warning signs she named in the interview, the plan reminds her what she said she'd do. Nana adds one short note. It is an option she can switch off.
