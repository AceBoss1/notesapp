🎨 Themes and kits: Nana sets up your site, and Review my site

Two additions to Your site. Nana asks a few questions about what you do, picks a kit and look, and drafts the words for each section; you keep, change or skip each one, and nothing goes on your site until you say so. Then Review my site lists what is stopping visitors from booking, buying or getting in touch, most important first, each with a button that takes you to the fix. Sample screens only: the example is a home baker in Enugu.

N How it works
What Nana does, and doesn't
✅Uses only what you told her and what is already in your shop, calendar and profile.
✅Shows every word before it goes on your site. You press the button.
✅Keeps your site as it was for 30 days, to put back.
🚫Never invents a price, a review, an award, a number or a claim about health or results.
🚫Never changes your site on her own, now or later.
🚫Never sends you to an outside tool for something #NotesApp does.
