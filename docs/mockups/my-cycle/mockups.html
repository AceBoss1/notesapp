N Ask Nana 🔒
Why do I get pimples before my period?
Nana
That's common. In the days before a period, hormone changes make the skin oilier, so pores block more easily. Wash gently twice a day, don't pick, and drink water. If it hurts a lot or leaves marks, a pharmacist or doctor can help. 🌸
