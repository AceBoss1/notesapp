# Startup Programmes: Where NotesApp Fits

As of 4 November 2026. The living copy, with Fit dropdowns the team can change, is the Claude Doc
[Startup Programmes: Where NotesApp Fits](https://claude.ai/artifact/FMtJntfGqTSXqz6i8njiRE). Investment programmes we've already
applied to or drafted for (Alliance, Village Global, Emergent Ventures, pitch.tech, startup.tech) are in `docs/FUNDING.md`.

## Summary

NotesApp can apply this week to seven programmes worth about $62,000 in credits plus Paystack's partner discounts, with no investor and no US company needed. The largest are PostHog ($50,000), Cloudflare ($10,000) and Google Cloud ($2,000). ElevenLabs, Notion and The Pitch by Deel's 2027 waitlist round it out.

- **Our edge:** NotesApp Technologies Ltd was registered on 2 September 2026, so we pass every "founded within 2 years" rule.
- **Next unlock:** forming NotesApp Inc, ideally through Stripe Atlas, opens Carta, Brex and Atlas's own perks bundle. Our US co-founders make Brex realistic. Mercury may still refuse while anyone living in Nigeria owns 25% or more.
- **Out of reach while bootstrapped:** Vercel, GitHub, Google's $200,000+ Scale tier, Cloudflare's $100,000+ tiers and Claude's extra $100,000 all need outside funding or a partner investor's referral.
- **Claude for Startups:** we're already in. Its official page says the cohort is full, so nothing more is on offer there now.
- **Caveat:** most rows rest on search results, not the vendors' own pages, because the research session's network blocked those sites. Each row says how it was checked.
